# MTE301 Final Project: Automatic Plant Care System
**School:** Toronto Metropolitan University
**Group Members**: Ashwin Sivanesan, Clark Velasco, Hadi Ali

**Project Description**  
Many plant enthusiasts do not have the time and energy to tend to their plants. This leads to their plants dying faster. Our design plans to water plants automatically without user intervention. This is done mainly using an Arduino UNO, a soil moisture detector and a water pump.
